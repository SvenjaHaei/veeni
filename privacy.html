What data I process and why

When you place an order: your name, email, phone number, delivery address and the contents of your order. I use them to process, ship and invoice your order and to contact you about it.

When you email me: your email address and what you write, to answer you.

Legal basis

  • Performing the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Meeting accounting and tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Your consent for the Meta pixel (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), see “Cookies and storage”.

Who receives the data

Only the services needed to run the shop:

  • Make (Celonis) – processes the order when you submit it.
  • Google (Google Sheets, Gmail) – order register and email.
  • Zásilkovna – name, phone, email and address, to deliver your parcel.
  • My bank – to match your payment.
  • GitHub Pages – hosts this website and briefly logs technical data such as IP addresses.
  • Google Sheets – the website reads a public list of sold items from Google (no personal data), which shares your IP address with Google.
  • Meta Platforms Ireland – only if you agree to the Meta pixel.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU; they do so under the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long I keep it

Order and invoice data for as long as Czech accounting and tax law requires. Emails as long as needed to deal with your request.

Cookies and storage

Your bag, chosen language and the details of your last order are stored only in your own browser (local storage) so the shop works; you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Only if you click “Accept” in the cookie notice, the website loads the Meta pixel from Meta Platforms Ireland. It sets cookies and tells Meta which pages you visit and when you add something to your bag, start the checkout or place an order (with the order value, not your name or address). I use this to measure whether my Instagram and Facebook posts and ads work. Meta may combine this with your Meta account and process it outside the EU. You can change your choice at any time under “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and a copy of your data, and you can object to processing. Write to info@veeni.cz. You can also file a complaint with the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (uoou.gov.cz).
