A workshop place is booked once the payment arrives. Bookings are binding: workshops take place on a fixed date, so the 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply (§ 1837 j) of the Czech Civil Code), and paid places are not refunded if you cancel.

If I have to cancel a workshop, you get the full price back, or a place at another date if you prefer.
